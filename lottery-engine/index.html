⚡ About the Quantum-Astrological Lottery Engine™

An elaborately over-engineered number picker that mixes simulated "quantum" stats, algorithmic astrology and personal numerology. It's for fun — every lottery draw is random.

🎯 Four quick steps

  • Choose your game — major jackpots, daily games (Keno, Pick 2/3/4), mega jackpots, or define your own custom game.
  • Choose how to pick — Quick Pick, Cosmic Blend, Hot Streak, Due Numbers, Personal or Reverse Field.
  • Customize (optional) — tap numbers to Must include, Never use or Track. Your choices are remembered per game.
  • Generate — up to 10 lines at once, then copy or save them.

🔧 Good to know

  • Must include = "complete my ticket": lock your favourites and let the engine fill the rest.
  • Digit games let you fix a digit to a specific position.
  • Category dots show which cosmic group a number belongs to.
  • Past winning numbers — paste recent results from your official results page. The app then uses real hot/cold stats, can avoid past winning lines, and checks your saved tickets against the matching draw.
  • Odds shown are the real mathematical odds for one line.
